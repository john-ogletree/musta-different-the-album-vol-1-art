## 📐 Overview & Concept

This repository exists strictly as a visual design sample and portfolio piece. The artwork pairs futuristic, cyber-styled typography with a double-exposure silhouette technique, filling a side-profile figure with a vibrant, neon-lit rainy streetscape watercolor texture.

* **Project Purpose:** Portfolio Piece / Album Cover Art Concept
* **GitHub Upload Date:** August 10, 2026
* **Asset File:** `musta the album vol 1.png`
* **Format:** Square High-Resolution PNG Image

## 🔍 Design Breakdown

* **Typography & Layout:**
  * **Artist Name:** `MUSTA` centered at the top in a minimalist sci-fi font
  * **Album Title:** `DIFFERENT` featured prominently in a futuristic, angular white display typeface across the upper third
  * **Subtitle:** `MUSTA THE ALBUM VOL 1` tracking cleanly along the bottom margin
* **Visual Elements:**
  * Central female profile silhouette with flowing hair and paint splatter accents
  * Double-exposure fill featuring a neon-lit urban street reflecting on rain-soaked pavement with watercolor and ink-wash textures
* **Color Palette:** Neon Magenta, Electric Pink, Emerald Green, Bright Cyan, Stark White, and a Textured Pitch-Black canvas
